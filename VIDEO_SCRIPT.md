## 🎙️ Spoken Video Script (Read in One Go)

"Hello everyone! My name is Akhil, and this is **Diligent**—an autonomous multi-agent operational engine designed to help small and medium businesses scale. We built Diligent because in India, over eighty-five percent of retail stores stay stuck in the small tier. Founders spend sixteen hours a day buried under billing counters, reordering stock by guesswork, and managing daily headaches with zero operational help.

Now, before building Diligent, let’s look at what happens when a store owner tries to use an everyday AI chatbot. They ask: *'What products should I reorder this week?'* And the chatbot fails immediately. It doesn't know what inventory came in yesterday, it doesn't know current stock levels, and it has zero memory of previous customer purchases. If you close the tab, everything is forgotten. Asking a store owner to copy-paste spreadsheet data into a generic prompt ten times a day simply does not work. 

Diligent solves this by giving small businesses an entire virtual operational team backed by persistent memory.

Right here on our main console, you see our operational team in action. Instead of one generic bot, Diligent runs specialized agents across our store divisions: POS billing, customer CRM, floor sales, and marketing. 

Watch what happens when I open our Floating Copilot by pressing Command-K. Notice this conversation history drawer. Diligent is connected to Supabase PostgreSQL and powered by high-speed Groq LPU inference. When I ask: *'Give me an operational recap of our store,'* the agent doesn’t ask for data. It instantly recalls our past inventory audit. It knows our catalog has ₹2.2 lakh in stock, identifies that ₹1.48 lakh is trapped in slow-moving items like handcrafted copper jugs, and flags our top-selling boAt earphones with over three hundred units billed this month.

Now let’s look at real-time product context. If I jump into the Inventory terminal, every single SKU has an 'Ask Agent' trigger. Watch this: when I click on our OnePlus Nord Buds, Diligent immediately attaches the product’s exact margins, safety buffers, and distributor lead times. It alerts me that stock is down to just four units against a reorder threshold of eight, and drafts a ready-to-send purchase order for our Bengaluru distributor with one click.

And it doesn't stop in English. Because retail staff in cities like Hyderabad speak regional languages, I can switch the copilot to Telugu or Hindi. Instantly, the agent explains customer upsell tactics and cashier scripts in clear regional phrasing that any store employee can use right on the shop floor.

Finally, we have our Executive Swarm. With one tap, all our subagents—inventory, CRM, and marketing—run an autonomous consensus loop. They reallocate marketing budget toward high-margin items, detect customers at risk of churning, and produce an actionable 30-60-90 day scaling roadmap.

What surprised us most while building Diligent is that memory is the bridge between a novelty chatbot and a true operational partner. When an AI remembers your business history, your inventory numbers, and your store staff, it transforms from a tool into a tireless Chief Operating Officer that actually protects your bottom line.

Thank you so much, and we look forward to presenting Diligent live at the Microsoft India Development Center in Hyderabad!"
