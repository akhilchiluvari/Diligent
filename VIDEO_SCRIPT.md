## Scene-by-Scene Script & Action Guide

### 🎬 Scene 1: The Hook & The Problem (0:00 – 0:18)
- **Visual:** Camera starts on presenter, cuts to an image of a bustling retail store in Madhapur with stacked inventory boxes, then transitions to Diligent's modern dark-mode dashboard.
- **On-Screen Text:** *"Why do 85% of Indian SMBs never scale?"*
- **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Over 85% of small and medium businesses in India never transition from the 'small' tier to medium or large scale. Why? Because merchants spend over 60% of their day trapped in operational firefighting—guessing stock levels, reconciling receipts, and tying up capital in dead inventory. They can't afford McKinsey consultants, and existing apps are just passive ledgers."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 2: Introducing Diligent & The Swarm (0:18 – 0:45)
- **Visual:** Screen shares the **Virtual Board of Directors** tab in Diligent. Mouse hovers over the 4 specialized agent cards with live pulsing telemetry dots.
- **On-Screen Text:** *"Diligent: Autonomous Multi-Agent Swarm powered by Grok LPU"*
- **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Meet Diligent: an autonomous Multi-Agent AI Consultant built for Hack with Hyderabad 3.0 at Microsoft. Rather than a generic chatbot, Diligent deploys a swarm of five specialized subagents powered by Grok's high-speed inference engine:
  > The Billing & Inventory Agent, the Sales & CRM Agent, the Strategy & Scaling Agent, the Web & Marketing Agent, all coordinated by our Orchestrator Supervisor."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 3: Live Barcode Ingestion & Telemetry (0:45 – 1:10)
- **Visual:** Switch to **Inventory & Barcode Scanner** tab. Click on preset barcode `8901030825010` (boAt Rockerz) and click *"Ingest Scan"*. Then click `8901233024018` (Amul Butter) and observe the red Stockout Hazard alert trigger instantly.
- **On-Screen Callout:** *"Real-Time POS Hardware Ingestion • Zero Delay"*
- **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Watch it in action. When a customer scans a barcode at checkout, Diligent ingests the transaction on the edge. It immediately calculates turnover velocity. Look here: it spots that Amul Butter has plunged to 8 units—projected to sell out in just 18 hours—and automatically drafts a supplier Purchase Order. Simultaneously, it detects over 1.48 lakh rupees locked in stagnant dead stock like these Nordic lamps."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 4: The Virtual Board of Directors (1:10 – 1:35)
- **Visual:** Click *"Convene Swarm Consensus Session"*. The agents deliberate in real time, and the Executive Growth Thesis card appears with a 98.4% alignment score. Toggle through Day 30, Day 60, and Day 90 roadmap tabs.
- **On-Screen Text:** *"Autonomous Consensus • Capital Reallocation Matrix"*
- **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Now, watch the Virtual Boardroom convene. In one second, the agents debate and synthesize a concrete SMB Scaling Directive.
  > It generates an exact Capital Reallocation plan: liquidate 48 units of stagnant lamps at a 25% discount to unlock ₹1,15,000, and immediately reinvest that cash into high-margin boAt audio accessories. Here is the custom 30, 60, and 90-day expansion blueprint."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 5: AI Marketing Studio (1:35 – 1:48)
- **Visual:** Switch to **AI Marketing Studio**. Select *"Dead Stock Clearance"* and click *"Generate Multi-Channel Campaign"*. Quickly preview the Instagram carousel caption, hashtags, and the live WhatsApp broadcast template.
- **On-Screen Text:** *"Inventory-Aware Content Generation"*
- **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Our Web & Marketing Agent connects directly to this inventory. With one click, it authors high-converting Instagram carousels, 15-second Reels scripts, and ready-to-broadcast WhatsApp messages tailored for Hyderabad tech professionals."*

---

### 🎬 Scene 6: Legal Gateway, Human-in-the-Loop & Closing (1:48 – 2:00)
- **Visual:** Click the *"Sign Liability Waiver"* pill to showcase the 4 compliance clauses, then click *"Approve"* on a safeguard action item, showing the confetti celebration. Return to camera.
- **On-Screen Text:** *"Strict Liability Abstraction • Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards"*
- **Voiceover Narration:**
  > *"Crucially, Diligent protects the business with strict legal liability abstraction and human-in-the-loop approval before any action goes live.
  > Built for Microsoft Hack with Hyderabad 3.0, Diligent turns every local merchant into an autonomous enterprise. Thank you!"*

## Quick Tips for Recording:
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at 1920x1080 (16:9).
2. **Audio:** Use a clean USB microphone or headset with minimal room echo.
3. **Browser:** Run in Full Screen mode (F11) with clean zoom level at 100%.
4. **Mouse pointer:** Enable a subtle pointer highlight so viewers can easily track clicks between tabs.
